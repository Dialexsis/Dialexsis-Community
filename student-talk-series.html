Student Talk Series

Share your journey.

A weekly student-led series where students can share their experiences of projects, internships, research work, or any other work they have been involved in.

01 — ABOUT THE SERIES

Everyone has a journey worth sharing.

The dialexsis Student Talk Series is a space where students can share the work they have been involved in and the journey behind it.

These sessions are semi-formal and interactive. The idea is not simply to present a project or an achievement, but to talk about the experiences, challenges, discoveries and lessons along the way.

Students may talk about projects, internships, research work, workshops, competitions, personal initiatives or any other experience they feel is worth sharing.

The series is open to everyone. You do not have to belong to a particular department or field to participate.

“
It is not only about what you did, but also about what you learned along the way.

— dialexsis Student Talk Series

02 — THE SERIES

What can you share?

01

Projects

Share a project you have worked on, whether academic, personal or collaborative.

02

Research

Talk about your research experience, questions you explored and what you learned from the process.

03

Internships

Tell us about your internship, the work you did and the experiences that stayed with you.

04

Other Experiences

Workshops, competitions, initiatives, experiments or anything else you think others might find interesting.

03 — PARTICIPATE

Want to share your story?

If you have something you would like to share with the community, we would love to hear from you.

The sessions are intended to be open, interactive and welcoming. You can share your work, your experiences, the problems you encountered and the things you learned.

You don't need to be an expert. You simply need to have something you would like to share.
