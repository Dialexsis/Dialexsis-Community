01 — ABOUT THE SERIES

Everyone has a journey worth sharing.

Dialexsis is starting a weekly Student Talk Series where students can share their experiences of projects, internships, research work, or any other work they have been involved in.

These sessions are semi-formal and interactive. The idea is not simply to present a project or an achievement, but to talk about the experiences, challenges, discoveries and lessons along the way.

Students can share their work, their experiences, the problems they encountered, and the things they learned along the way.

The series is open to everyone. You do not need to belong to a particular department or field to participate.

“
It is not only about what you did, but also about what you learned along the way.

— dialexsis Student Talk Series
