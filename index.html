01 — INTRODUCTION

What is dialexsis?

dialexsis is a student-led space created for conversation, curiosity and the exchange of ideas.

It began with a simple thought: students need spaces where they can talk freely, ask questions and explore ideas beyond the boundaries of a formal classroom.

dialexsis brings students together for discussions, talks, experiences and conversations. The aim is not simply to provide answers, but to create an environment where asking a good question is valued just as much as knowing an answer.

Whether the discussion is about science, research, philosophy, society, personal experiences or simply an interesting question, everyone is welcome to participate.

“
A conversation can begin with a question and end somewhere neither of us expected.

— The spirit of dialexsis
